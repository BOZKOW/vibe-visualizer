Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.19.3 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.19.2 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### New

- **A warning when the phone gets warm**, over the whole screen, offering Low performance mode (with the battery's temperature). When it appears: while the app is on screen, as soon as Android says the phone has started slowing itself down because of heat ("getting warm"), or predicts it will within about 10 seconds; "Your phone is hot" when Android slows it down hard. After "Not now" it waits 20 minutes and until the phone has cooled down; the hot warning still comes. It's part of Settings → Performance → Suggest Low performance mode when needed (turn that off and it never shows)
- **Previous and next animate**: the arrows slide out the way you skip and spring back in. **Play / pause turns and springs** as it swaps

### Changes

- Bigger controls on the Lyrics screen and the Visualizer: larger previous, play / pause and next, and larger round buttons

From 0.19.2: Settings over a live blur of the player, and the top of the screen gets the same gradient as behind the controls over a Canvas.
