Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.1.2 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.1.1 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### What's new

- Quick settings: Settings (in the ⋮ menu, or the app shortcut) first opens a panel that floats over the player, which blurs behind it, as One UI 9's quick panel does. Tiles for translation, romanization, the bars, Canvas, Blur the Canvas, the smaller cover, keep the screen on, picture-in-picture and low performance; thick sliders for the lyrics size, the background brightness and the Canvas blur; the design; lyrics timing and another source. The gear (or All settings) opens the full Settings
- One UI is now One UI 9: Settings' first screen has no title bar, the app's own card on top, the pages without descriptions and the search bar floating at the bottom, in reach of your thumb; the player has round play, back and skip buttons, a thicker seek bar, a thick volume slider and a floating bar at the bottom; cards are a little less round

### Fixes

- Your server (Settings → Help & about): the address, the Test and save button and its answer were drawn over each other. Every row like it in Settings now lays its parts out one under another
