Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.0.3 (beta): the Canvas works, and notices behave like Android's. Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.0.2.

### What's new

- Spotify Canvas videos play again: in the cover, or across the whole screen with "Hide the cover while a Canvas plays", with the blurred Canvas behind everything
- Short messages are a snackbar at the bottom, one at a time, swipe to dismiss; most are gone, because what they said is already on screen (the heart, the switches, the device)
- Update available, lyrics out of sync and battery saver are a snackbar with one button; questions you asked for (allow installs, forget this device, reconnect Spotify) are a dialog
- Problems no longer cover the lyrics: with lyrics on screen they're a short line under the song; without, an empty state with an icon, a few words and one button (Open Tailscale, Turn off Private DNS, Connect, Try again)
- "No lyrics" and "Couldn't load lyrics" are shorter, with Look again, Add a file or Try again; "Looking for lyrics…" is a small loading indicator
