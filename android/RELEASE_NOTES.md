Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.0.6 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.0.5 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### What's new

- While a Canvas plays, the cover glides to a smaller size in the middle, so more of the Canvas shows behind it; it grows back when the song has no Canvas
- From 0.0.5: song changes show at once when you skip in the Spotify app on this phone
