Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.19.2 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.19.1 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Changes

- **Settings sit over the player, blurred live**: the lyrics, the bars and the Canvas go on moving behind a dimmed blur instead of a plain background (both designs). In Low performance mode Settings cover the player as before
- **The top of the screen gets the same gradient as behind the controls** over a Canvas: as dark at the top as at the bottom, and the video softens into the blurred Canvas there too (Blur behind the controls already frosts both)

From 0.19.1: Settings search without exact matches.
