Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.19.7 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.19.6 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Fixes

- **Landscape keeps clear of the camera hole**: the cover and the rest no longer run into it. The app now goes by where the hole really is (with a little room beside it), not only by the space the phone reports for it, which on some phones is less than the hole takes on its side. Settings too
- Copy debug info (Settings → Lyrics cache) now includes the camera cutout's numbers, in case a phone still gets it wrong

From 0.19.6: Low performance mode with Power saving, closing the app ends it, and the top's blur over a Canvas only while something's there.
