Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.24.0 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.23.1 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Changed

- **The app has its own home now: [BOZKOW/vibe-visualizer-android](https://github.com/BOZKOW/vibe-visualizer-android)**, apart from the web app's repository. Updates and *What's new* come from there from this version on; nothing changes on the phone, your settings and saved lyrics stay. (Versions up to 0.23.1 are on the [main repository's Releases page](https://github.com/BOZKOW/vibe-visualizer/releases).)

From 0.23.1: Settings over a live blur of the player again, and smooth.
