Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.1.6 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.1.5 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### What's new

- Picture-in-picture has controls: previous, play / pause and next on the small window (tap it once to show them)

### Glass, fixed

- Glassier: the cards are frosted glass with a shiny edge, bright along the top, instead of dark boxes; the seek bar and the buttons sit on a frosted panel on Now Playing, Lyrics, the Visualizer and wide screens
- Easier to read: Settings darkens the blurred cover behind it more, and the headings and notes between the cards are white with a soft shadow (they were faint, or in the cover's color, on the cover)
- Not broken: the search field is glass too instead of a solid block, and the floating bar at the bottom is only as wide as its icons, in the middle (on a phone on its side it was squeezed and its icons were cut off)
