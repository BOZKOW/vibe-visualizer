Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.1.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.1.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Fixes

- Covers: the online search took the first result as it came, so another song's cover often showed. Now only the same song counts (its title and artist), and before going online the app asks Android: your own songs get the album art the phone has for them (their picture, or a cover.jpg / folder.jpg next to them), and Spotify songs without a cover from Spotify get the one the Spotify app on this phone shows
- While the music plays on this phone, the bars no longer follow Vibe Bridge on a computer (it hears that computer, not the phone), and the hints no longer tell you to start it there

From 0.1.0: the One UI design (Settings → Appearance → Design), duet lines at the right edge, and a soft edge between the Canvas and the blurred Canvas on wide screens.
