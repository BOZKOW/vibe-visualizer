Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.19.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.1.19 (Settings → Help & about → Update now). From now on, a big feature bumps the middle number (0.20.0) and small fixes the last one (0.19.2…).

### Fixes

- **Searching Settings doesn't need the exact words any more**: they can come in any order or be half typed, a typo or two is fine (a letter wrong, missing, extra or swapped), accents and dashes don't matter, and "lockscreen" finds "lock screen". A setting also matches by its section and page ("widget size" finds the widget's Lyrics size), and the closest matches come first

From 0.1.19: tapping the top of the Lyrics screen no longer closes the lyrics.
