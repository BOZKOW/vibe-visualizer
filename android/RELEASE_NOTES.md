Vibe Visualizer for Android 0.23.1 (beta). Android 12 or newer; it installs over 0.23.0 (Settings → Help & about → Update now).

### Changes

- **Settings are a blurred layer over the player again**, live (the lyrics, bars and Canvas go on moving under them), and this time smooth: the player is drawn into a picture an eighth of the size, blurred there and stretched back over the screen. A heavy blur looks the same, with about 60 times fewer pixels to blur every frame (blurring the full screen is what made Settings stutter). The sheets in Glass blur the same cheaper way. In Low performance mode Settings cover the player instead

From 0.23.0: the app follows other music apps (YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Apple Music…).
